# The fractional and signed packing equations at height 53

A four-box coloring uses 53 minimum layers. Every colored
rectangle slot occurs once, with one additional occurrence on each of
four prescribed colored row pairs. Up to relabeling, the latter pairs are
\(01,02,12\) in red and \(34\) in blue. For the equations below each of the four
column pairs ranges independently over the 21 edges of \(K_7\).

Let \(\mathcal L\) be the 4,384,800 labeled minimum layers and let \(A\)
be the \(420\times|\mathcal L|\) matrix whose column for \(L\) indicates
its eight occupied colored rectangle slots. For any of the \(21^4\)
assignments, write \(b=\mathbf1+\sum_{t\in D}e_t\), where \(D\) is the
resulting set of four slots.

**Proposition.** Each of these right-hand sides has both of the following
properties.

1. The system \(Ax=b\) has a rational solution with
   \(1/(4\cdot83520)<x_L<65/83520\) for every \(L\).
2. It has an integral solution with possibly negative coefficients.

In each case \(\sum_Lx_L=53\), since each column has sum eight and \(b\)
has sum 424. Thus all geometric placements survive the full linear
relaxation \(0\le x_L\le1\) and all linear congruences of the incidence
equations. The conditions together impose no sign or integrality guarantee
on a common solution. The proof and exact computation follow.

## The integral affine lattice

Use the two minimum layers
\[
 a=(7,11,12,17,17,22,26),\qquad b'=(5,9,10,17,19,22,28).
\]
Their ten row translates are indexed \(0,\ldots,9\), taking the five
cyclic shifts of \(a\) followed by the five of \(b'\). Take five copies
of all ten, and one additional copy of layers 0, 3 and 6. After exchanging
row labels 2 and 4, the resulting 53 layers have label totals 21, with
one extra on each of the four required colored row pairs. This assertion
involves only the row labels and is checked by counting rectangles.

Exchanging the last two columns of \(b'\) changes a single occupied slot.
Its row pair and red color stay fixed, and its column pair moves from
\(45\) to \(46\). Independent permutations of rows and columns and
a global color swap therefore realize, as a difference of two layer
vectors, every difference between adjacent column edges of the same
colored row pair. The line graph of \(K_7\) is connected. Integer sums
of these moves realize every change whose 21 coordinates sum to zero
on each colored row pair. Apply the moves to the 53-layer multiset to
obtain any prescribed \(b\), retaining the total coefficient sum.

### The full lattice at any height

For an integer slot vector \(z\), let \(r_{ij,c}\) be its sum over the
21 column pairs of the label \((ij,c)\). There is an integer \(x\) with
\(Ax=z\) and \(\sum_Lx_L=h\), allowing negative coefficients, if and only if
\[
 \sum_{ij,c}r_{ij,c}=8h,\qquad
 r_{ij,1}-r_{ij,0}=u_i+u_j-h\quad(i<j)
\]
for some \(u_1,\ldots,u_5\in\mathbb Z\). In particular the formula
describes all additive congruence obstructions for minimum layers.

To prove necessity in one layer, let \(w_i\) be the number of red bits in
row \(i\). The colored row pair agrees once plus its rectangle count in
each color. Subtracting gives \(r_{ij,1}-r_{ij,0}=w_i+w_j-7\). Take
\(u_i=w_i-3\) and add the identities with their integer coefficients.

For sufficiency, subtract \(h\) copies of any one layer. On the 20
label totals we now want to generate the lattice difference at height
zero. Let \(V\) be the real space of vectors \(y\) whose total is zero
and whose label differences are \(v_i+v_j\) for some real \(v_i\).
It has dimension 14, since the ten pair sums and the five vertex
parameters are otherwise free. Put \(\Lambda=V\cap\mathbb Z^{20}\).
For \(y\in\Lambda\), the vertex parameters are either all integers or
all half-integers with odd numerator. This follows by subtracting
pair differences and using \(2v_i=(v_i+v_j)+(v_i+v_k)-(v_j+v_k)\).
Both cases occur. For the second, take red coordinate 1 on any five
edges and blue coordinate \(-1\) on the other five, with all remaining
coordinates zero. The integral-vertex subgroup thus has index two.

Let \(\Gamma\) be generated by differences of actual minimum-layer
label signatures. It lies in that index-two subgroup. The verification
script exhibits 15 realizable signatures. Subtract their first from
each of the other 14 and restrict to 14 specified label coordinates.
The resulting square matrix has determinant 2. Projection to these
coordinates is injective on \(V\), so the lattice generated by the 14
differences has index at most two in \(\Lambda\). It follows that
\(\Gamma\) is exactly the integral-vertex subgroup. Finally apply the
within-label column-edge moves above to reach the individual slots of
\(z\). This proves the equivalence.

## A positive rational solution

Put \(G=AA^\mathsf T\). For a pair of slots the entry of \(G\) depends
only on the triple
\[
 (\text{row-edge intersection size},\
   \text{indicator of equal colors},\
   \text{column-edge intersection size}).
\]
These are 18 orbits under the row, column and color symmetries. Number
them lexicographically. Their entries, in that order, are
\[
 (528,2304,10320,\ 2352,0,0,\ 2256,0,0,\ 960,2736,360,\
  3888,0,0,\ 0,0,83520).
\]
For a direct count, let \(n_i\) be the size of the \(i\)-th layer orbit
among the six classified minimum-layer types. Multiply the number of
ordered pairs of occupied slots in relation \(k\) in its representative
by \(n_i\) and sum over the six. Divide by the number of ordered slot
pairs in relation \(k\). In particular each slot is covered exactly
\(g=83520\) times, so \(x_L=1/g\) gives \(Ax=\mathbf1\).
The six representatives can be checked by the
[minimum-layer classification](classify_minimal_layers.py).

Fix a reference slot \(t\), and let \(P_k(t)\) consist of all slots in
relation \(k\) to \(t\). For any vector constant on these sets,
multiplication by \(G\) is multiplication of its 18 coefficients by
\[
 M_{ij}=\sum_{v\in P_j(t)}G_{uv},\qquad u\in P_i(t).
\]
Denote the above 18 Gram entries by the column vector \(g_0\). Solve
\(M^2 y=g_0\) by rational Gaussian elimination. For any slot \(t\),
write \(y^{(t)}_u=y_{\mathrm{rel}(u,t)}\). The calculation checks two
identities for \(G y^{(t)}-e_t\). Its value at \(u\) is independent of the
column-edge intersection, and the sum of these values over the four
\(t\in D\) vanishes at every \(u\). These 18-coordinate and 20-label
tests give \(\sum_{t\in D}G y^{(t)}=\sum_{t\in D}e_t\) for all \(21^4\)
choices of column pairs. Consequently
\[
 x_L=\frac1g+\sum_{t\in D}\sum_{u\in L}y_{\mathrm{rel}(u,t)}
 \qquad (L\in\mathcal L)
\]
solves the incidence equations. Here \(u\in L\) means that \(L\)
contains slot \(u\). Solving the squared system is one way of obtaining
the coefficients; the residual identities check the formula directly.

For the lower bound, minimize \(gx_L\) over the four column-pair choices
independently for each fixed layer \(L\). Permuting its columns preserves
this minimum, leaving just the 900 row-labeled column multisets to
check. The least value is
\[
 \frac{43584131462153440615235360669}
      {154145247127369077787605523125}>\frac14.
\]
The upper bound follows from \(\max_k|g y_k|<2\) and the 32 summands.
All counts, the reduced linear solve, the residual identities, and the
weight bounds use integer or rational arithmetic in the
[verification script](verify_fractional_53.py). Run
\(\texttt{python3 verify_fractional_53.py}\) to reproduce them.

## Testing a restricted pool over \(\mathbb F_2\)

The full-lattice result leaves an effective exact test for a restricted
set of candidate layers. Let \(V\) be the binary span of their slot
vectors, and write \([x]\) for reduction modulo \(V\). The target with
doubled slots \(s_1,\ldots,s_4\) requires
\[
 [e_{s_1}]+[e_{s_2}]=[\mathbf1]+[e_{s_3}]+[e_{s_4}] .
\]
There are 20 choices for the high-weight rows before identifying color
reversals. For each choice, tabulate the \(21^2\) sums on the left and
compare the \(21^2\) on the right. This counts exactly how many of the
\(20\cdot21^4=3,889,620\) target profiles belong to the binary span.

The published 263-layer witness cannot contain a 53-layer four-box
substack. Its 263 slot vectors are linearly independent modulo two,
and exactly one target profile survives. The full witness has 416 slots
of multiplicity five and four of multiplicity six, giving precisely
that parity profile. Independence makes the set of all 263 layers its
unique binary preimage.

For comparison, permit each source layer to undergo one transposition of
columns which moves at most one occupied slot, and pool all resulting
layers with all originals. The table gives the number of surviving
profiles, allowing arbitrary subsets of the resulting pool.

| Source height | Original rank | Original profiles | Enlarged pool size | Enlarged rank | Enlarged profiles |

after subtracting the fixed layers from the target.

### Classification of all single-slot trades

Join two different minimum layers when they share at least seven of
their eight occupied slots. This graph is the disjoint union of
1,965,600 isolated vertices, 604,800 edges and 302,400 four-cycles.
In particular every such move is a transposition of two columns in
the selected layer.

To verify the classification, fix a representative of each of the
six minimum-layer orbits. Any neighbor must share at least seven
colored row-pair labels. Filter the 900 column multisets by this
condition and apply all column permutations to the survivors.
The numbers of distinct neighbors, in the representative order
of the [projection check](check_column_projections.py), are
\(0,0,1,0,2,0\). Every neighbor found has exactly seven slots in
common and is a column transposition of its seed. The one-neighbor
orbit has 1,209,600 layers. The two-neighbor orbit also has 1,209,600
layers, and its two switches independently generate a closed set of
four layers. Symmetry transports these conclusions to all labeled
layers. The [binary elimination check](verify_pool_parity.py) also
performs this separate neighborhood enumeration.

Repeated transpositions moving a single occupied slot enlarge the last
pool from 263 to 709 and then to 919 distinct layers. The 919-layer set
is closed under every such transposition. Exhaustive search shows that
it still contains no 53-layer four-box substack, even though its binary
span contains every candidate target.

The search fixes the high-weight row set and requires 22 assignments
on each of its four distinguished labels, and 21 on the other labels.
At each node it chooses an uncovered slot with the fewest compatible
layers and branches over all those layers. A used slot on an ordinary
label excludes all other layers containing it. Once a distinguished
label has been repeated, every used slot on that label also excludes
all further incidences. Each layer supplies at most one slot on any
one label, so a label needing more assignments than remaining layers
rejects the branch; a label needing exactly that many must be present
in every remaining layer. Acceptance requires 53 selected layers
covering all 420 slots. Every valid completion is represented in one
of the branches on the chosen uncovered slot.

The [standard-library C++ verifier](check_switched_pool.cpp) builds the
919 layers from the original certificate and exhausts all 20 row sets.
From the repository root, compile with
\(\texttt{c++ -O3 -std=c++17 check_switched_pool.cpp -o /tmp/check_switched_pool}\)
and run \(\texttt{/tmp/check_switched_pool 5x7_multiplicity_certificates.txt}\).
This rules out compressing this witness by any sequence of the specified
switches and deletions. The [four-box construction](four_box_construction.md) uses layers outside this pool.
